# THE LAST DROP: Story Bible

The single source of truth for the story. **Update this file with every change to
story, characters, regions, monsters or events** (dialogue lives in
`data/dialogue.json` and `data/npcs.json`; this file says why it is there).

Tone: a quiet, warm post-collapse story in the spirit of Stardew Valley's small
kindnesses, the found-family of *Nausicaä* and *Mushishi*, and the slow reveals
of a light novel. Danger is real but the heart of it is people rebuilding.
Lines are short, present tense, never a lecture.

---

## 1. The premise

The world was not destroyed in a day. It was buried, one thrown-away thing at a
time. Cities sank under their waste, rivers turned the colour of old tea, and
the dead began to walk: people too sick from the poison in the ground and water
to stay people. The survivors call them *the Spoiled*.

You wake in the ruins of an outpost with a cracked recorder. A woman's voice,
**Ines Varga**, says: *follow the old pipeline north, to the Last Drop.* Everyone
has heard of it: a spring of pure water. It is not a spring.

## 2. Themes

1. **Waste is a resource in the wrong place.** Every region teaches one system.
2. **No single villain.** Every region offers a culprit; each is partly right.
3. **Repair is relational.** You cannot fix the world alone: every system you
   restore needs someone you met to keep it running.
4. **Memory.** What we forget, we repeat.

## 5. The main plot (chapter by chapter)

**Ch.0 Outpost.** Wake, find water, learn sorting, repair the treatment unit.
Meet **Tobi** (gives you the rusty sword from his chest and the first shard
quest) and **Mira** (wants a radio valve). Light the generator. Recording 1.

**Ch.1A Battery Factory.** Hazardous waste. Meet **Dr. Halden**, who thinks
he poisoned the valley. The archive shows budgets from far away (false villain
1). He gives you the **Ember rune** (flamethrower) to burn contaminated sludge
blocking the lab. Radio valve found here.

**Ch.1B Great Dump.** Meet **Nana Ru** and **Kip**. Sort three heaps, rebuild a
shelter, race Kip. Nana trades the **Scatter rune** (shotgun) for perfect piles.
Seed tin found here. The municipal records: everyone's waste had an address.

**Ch.2 Dead River.** Test the water, build the filter chain. **Osei** turns out
to be the voice on Mira's radio. He knew Ines: "She is not waiting for you.
She left you instructions." Filter cartridge found here.

**Ch.3 Silent Fields.** The soil forgot how to live. **Lena** has given up;
**Jory** shows you the compost heap he keeps secretly. Blight Husks rise from
poisoned crops. Restore the compost cycle and the first sprout grows; Lena
smiles for the first time in the game. Recording 4.

**Ch.4 Forgotten Industry.** Smog, hazard symbols, the energy grid. **Vex**
holds the generators; **Unit-7** sweeps. Vex sets his **Slag Golem** (a machine
packed with industrial waste) on you, or listens if you have helped enough
people. Restore the grid (efficiency first). Recording 5.

**Ch.5 Last Sanctuary.** Moss Sentinels guard a dark atrium. Power it with the
grid, and **Warden Moss** opens the archive. Six fragments, one per region,
reveal there was no single catastrophe. Ines's final recording. If you returned
all of Tobi's shards, **Tobi** arrives: he remembers.

**Ch.6 The Choice.** Preserve or Share. The epilogue reflects your sorting, the
systems you restored and the people you helped.

## 6. Side stories and events

- **Tobi's memory shards** (one per region): photos he half-recognises. Bring
  each back to him (gates back) for a line of his past and a reward. All found:
  he joins the ending.
- **Errands** (in game): radio valve (Factory to Outpost), seed tin (Dump to
  Outpost garden), filter cartridge (River to Factory fountain).
- **Kip's race** (Dump): sort a heap before Kip does.
- **Night of the Spoiled** (event): when a region's generator starts, a final
  wave tests the light.
- **Unit-7's last log** (Sanctuary): found after the ending choice.

## 8. Runes (special abilities)

Runes are old maintenance tools the survivors repurposed. Each lives in an
ability slot (keys **1**, **2**, **3**) and charges with **energy** (the purple
bar), which refills over time and with kills.

| Rune | Found | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | Dr. Halden, Factory | Flamethrower: a cone of fire that sets the Spoiled ablaze (burning damage over time, spreads to neighbours). |
| **Scatter** | Nana Ru, Dump | Shotgun: a spread of pellets with heavy knockback. |
| **Tide** | Osei, River | A wave of clean water: heals you and pushes everything back. |
